## Why

source-specific production contracts remain unaccepted.

fixtures, bounded live evidence, immutable artifact proof and test-only API
readback. Do not register, schedule, deploy or promote any source.

GOV-SPEC-005 and GOV-SPEC-006. Production behavior remains blocked.
