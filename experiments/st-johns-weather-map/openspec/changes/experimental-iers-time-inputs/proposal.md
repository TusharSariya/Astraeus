# Experimental IERS time inputs and NAIF LSK

## Why

Earth-orientation and civil-time inputs are catalogued research gaps, but no accepted source contract authorizes their use in astronomy science.

## What

Add bounded isolated readers for IERS finals2000A, IERS Leap_Second.dat, and pinned NAIF0012. Preserve every published field, unit, status, version, expiry and UTC identity through immutable artifacts and an experimental HTTP readback. Do not register, schedule, admit, derive science, or select an Earth PCK.

Spec-Impact: experiment only; no production or normative promotion.
Spec-Refs: GOV-SPEC-001, GOV-SPEC-002, GOV-SPEC-004, GOV-SPEC-005, GOV-SPEC-006
