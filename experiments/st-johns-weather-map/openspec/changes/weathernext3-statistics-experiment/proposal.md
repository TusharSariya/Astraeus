# WeatherNext 3 statistics experiment

## Why

The approved account sample proves bounded access to WeatherNext 3 statistics,
but no source-specific contract states what an isolated adapter may preserve or
what remains blocked.

## Scope

Add an unregistered experimental adapter, a complete field disposition, and
fixture, live-evidence, immutable-artifact, Astraeus-readback and failure tests.
This draft does not register or schedule the source, mark it operational, allow
future-data redistribution, or change normative status.
