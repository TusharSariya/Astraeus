# Experimental NL provincial air-quality observations

## Why

The St. John's provincial rolling CSV is the only timely measured PM2.5 and
ozone source in the evidence box, but it is provisional, unvalidated and under
restricted copyright. Its exact source contract is not accepted.

## What

Add an isolated, bounded adapter and tiny fixture for the selected PM2.5
24-hour running average and ozone mole fraction. Preserve every upstream
column's disposition, local-time identity, provisional quality, restricted
terms and `operational: false`. Prove an immutable artifact and actual HTTP API
readback without scheduler registration or raw evidence in Git.

Spec-Impact: experiment only; no production or normative promotion.
